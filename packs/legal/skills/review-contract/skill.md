# Review Contract

Use this skill to prepare a draft contract review against the tenant legal playbook.

Process:

1. Identify contract type, parties, user side, deadline, and focus areas.
2. Read the whole document before classifying issues.
3. Extract material clauses with source references.
4. Compare clauses against the legal playbook and risk policy.
5. Classify issues as green, yellow, or red.
6. Draft suggested language for negotiation points when the playbook supports it.
7. Mark all outputs as draft-only and route red or uncertain findings to human legal approval.

Never approve, sign, send, or present the analysis as legal advice.
