# Triage NDA

Use this skill to prepare a draft NDA screening report.

Screen for:

- Mutuality and document type
- Confidential information scope
- Required carveouts
- Permitted disclosures
- Term and survival
- Return or destruction obligations
- Remedies
- Embedded non-solicit, non-compete, exclusivity, standstill, IP transfer, audit, or penalty language
- Governing law and dispute forum

Classify as green, yellow, or red only as a routing recommendation. Human legal approval remains required before signature.
