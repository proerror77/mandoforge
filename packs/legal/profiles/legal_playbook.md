# Legal Playbook

## Contract Review Positions

- Liability: prefer a mutual aggregate cap at 12 months of fees paid or payable. Yellow if the cap is 6-12 months of fees or has narrow market-standard carveouts. Red if liability is uncapped, excludes only one party, or has broad carveouts that make the cap ineffective.
- Indemnity: prefer mutual third-party indemnity for IP infringement, confidentiality breach, data breach, and gross negligence or willful misconduct. Red if the tenant must indemnify for the counterparty's conduct, if indemnity is uncapped without approval, or if defense-control rights are missing.
- Data protection: require a DPA before processing personal data. Require sub-processor notice, breach notification within 72 hours where GDPR may apply, deletion or return on termination, and transfer safeguards for cross-border processing.
- IP ownership: each party keeps pre-existing IP. Customer data remains customer-owned. Deliverables may be licensed for the business purpose, but broad work-for-hire, feedback assignment, or ownership transfer requires legal approval.
- Termination: prefer termination for cause with a cure period and termination for convenience where business risk requires flexibility. Red if renewal is automatic without a workable notice period or if data return, transition, or survival terms are missing.
- Governing law: prefer Delaware, New York, California, or England and Wales for commercial agreements. Non-standard jurisdictions, mandatory arbitration without review, or remote venue clauses require legal approval.

## NDA Defaults

- Mutuality: require mutual obligations unless the business context is strictly one-way disclosure by the counterparty.
- Confidentiality term: prefer 2-3 years for ordinary confidential information and longer protection only for trade secrets or regulated information.
- Required carveouts: public knowledge, prior possession, independent development, rightfully received third-party information, and legally compelled disclosure with notice when allowed.
- Prohibited clauses: non-compete, broad non-solicit, exclusivity, standstill, IP assignment, audit rights, liquidated damages, penalty language, or broad residuals clauses.

## Escalation

Any red classification, missing source evidence, or non-standard jurisdiction requires human legal approval.

## Output Boundary

The pack may prepare clause summaries, routing recommendations, risk flags, and draft fallback language. It must not provide final legal advice, approve documents, request signatures, send counterparty messages, or mutate external legal systems.
