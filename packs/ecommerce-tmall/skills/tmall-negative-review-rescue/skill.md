# Tmall Negative Review Rescue

Turn negative reviews and damaging follow-up reviews into rescue work items.
Link review VOC, order context, after-sales status, product batch clues, and
customer-service history.

Output three separated artifacts:

- Public merchant explanation draft.
- Private recovery plan.
- Internal root-cause/prevention note.

Track rescue state from detection through follow-up. Do not blame buyers, expose
private order details, or submit explanations without approval.
